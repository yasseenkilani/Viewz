Before you send a listing

Good questions.

Do you need to visit the property?

No. We work from listing photos and the relevant floor plan. If more material is needed, we’ll tell you during the review.

Is the walkthrough actual footage?

No. It’s an AI-generated interpretation checked against the materials you provide. We label the finished film as AI-generated.

Who can commission a film?

Agents, owners and holiday-home hosts can submit a listing. For off-plan units, we review the developer’s drawings and reference imagery before confirming what can be shown.

What if my listing has few photos?

We check every walkthrough against the floor plan and your listing photos. If we can’t verify a room, we leave it out.

What does it cost and how long does it take?

Both depend on the property and the available material. Your quote gives a fixed price and a delivery date before you approve anything.

What files do I receive?

A landscape walkthrough and a vertical version for short-form sharing. We agree the title card and branding with you before production.
