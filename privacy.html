Privacy

Your details, plainly.

What we collect, why, who handles it, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Draft for owner approval. Items in [square brackets] are proposals to be confirmed before launch. This page is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

Viewz makes AI-generated walkthrough videos of Dubai property from a listing link, the developer's floor plan and the listing photos. You can reach us at dev@viewz.me.

What we collect

  • The listing link you send us.
  • Your name and WhatsApp number, so we can reply and send you the price, delivery date and route.
  • Your email, only if you choose to give it.
  • Order details: the quote, its price, its status and the delivery formats.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp.

We do not collect your card details. When you pay, you enter them on our payment provider's page, never on this site. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Why we use it

  • To quote, confirm your number (we send a 6-digit code on WhatsApp), make the video and deliver it.
  • To take payment and keep a record of it.
  • To answer questions about your order.

We contact you only about your own request. We never sell your details and we do not use them for unrelated marketing without asking you first.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries our messages to you.
  • Stripe, which takes card payments and sends us a confirmation.
  • Our hosting and database providers [to list once chosen].

These providers may process data outside the UAE. We share only what each one needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

  • Quote requests that never became an order: [90 days], then deleted.
  • Orders and payment records: [7 years], as accounting records are usually required.
  • Delivered videos and the listing material: [12 months after delivery], then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email dev@viewz.me from the address or WhatsApp number you used and we will reply within [30 days]. We may need to keep payment records for the period above.

Security

This site sends your details over an encrypted connection. Payment is handled on a secure page run by our payment provider. Access to your details is limited to the people who need it to fulfil your order.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date above.
